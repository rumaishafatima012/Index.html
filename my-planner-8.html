Accountability System
Your Personal Rules
These are non-negotiable. Not goals. Rules.
01
The 2-Day Rule
You can miss a habit once. Never twice in a row. Missing twice is choosing to quit. One miss is human. Two is a pattern.
02
Sunday is Review Day
Every Sunday, fill the weekly review. No skipping. If you don't review, you don't learn. You just repeat.
03
Quran Before Phone
Every single morning. This one habit protects your intention for the whole day. Break this and the day starts reactive.
04
One Business Hour is Sacred
One hour daily. Non-negotiable. Even on bad days. Even when you don't feel like it. Especially then.
05
Month-End Honest Audit
Last day of every month: fill the monthly review with brutal honesty. What you measure you improve. What you ignore you repeat.
06
Niyyah First
Before starting any work session — 10 seconds. What is this for? Allah. Your future. Your sadqa e jariyah. That's why it matters.
